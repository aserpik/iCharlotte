Background
Defendant Philip Michael Houlihan was born on January 11, 1948, in Galesburg, Illinois. He has resided at 4125 Alpine Road, Portola Valley, CA 94028, for more than five years. Defendant has no felony convictions and speaks, reads, and writes English with ease. He graduated from Costa High School in 1966, received a B.S. in Finance from Southern Illinois University in 1970, and earned an MBA in Finance and Marketing from the University of Arizona in approximately 1972. Defendant operates as an individual doing business as Creekside Group Properties.
Employment
Defendant has been retired for more than five years.
Incident Details
Defendant was not present at the time of the occurrence and did not witness the incident. He is not aware of any reports, citations, or charging documents related to the incident. Defendant is not making any claim for damages.
Witnesses and Evidence
Defendant identifies Plaintiff Patricia Mary Campi, Patti Murdock, and various first responders as individuals with knowledge of the incident. Defendant possesses a diagram of the property at Highway 140 and 7th Street, Mariposa, California. Documentary evidence includes a 2003 newspaper photograph of Donuts A Go Go, thirteen inspection photographs from January 8, 2019, by John K. Burton, and text/email correspondence with Caleb Scott Chaney and Plaintiff. Defendant holds a business mercantile policy with State Farm General Insurance Company (Policy No. 90-E5-K636-7) with liability limits of $2,000,000 per occurrence. The named insureds are Philip M. and Christine M. Houlihan. Tenants were required to, and initially did, include Defendant on their own business insurance policy.
Other Claims
Defendant asserts affirmative defenses based on his right to a general denial and the burden of proof, noting that he currently lacks sufficient information to fully respond while discovery is ongoing.
Third-Party Liability
Defendant contracted Caleb Scott Chaney, dba Caleb Chaney Painting and Roofing, to perform various tasks at the premises starting in November 2022. These tasks included finishing the building exterior, replacing the walkway overhang, removing non-functional AC and electrical equipment, sealing leaks, completing building interiors, and waterproofing the donut shop roof.
