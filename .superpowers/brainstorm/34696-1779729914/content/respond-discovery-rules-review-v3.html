Objection Rules

Include "vague, ambiguous, overbroad" objections

Include ambiguous term objection when the discovery request contains word(s) that are confusing or not obviously clear

The agent identifies the unclear term and inserts it into the undefined-term objection, including the Code Civ. Proc. section reference.

Include relevance and privacy objections when Discovery asks for information not related to the case

Adds the relevance / privacy objection when the request appears unrelated to the case or invades Responding Party's privacy.

Include burdensome objections when the discovery asks for a lot of information

Adds the undue burden / oppression objection with the Code Civ. Proc. and Columbia Broadcasting citations.

Include privilege objections when the discovery asks for potentially privileged information

Adds the attorney-client privilege and/or attorney work-product objection when the request could seek protected information.

Include Expert Opinion or Legal Conclusion objections when Request calls for potential legal conclusion or expert opinion

Adds the expert opinion / legal conclusion objection when the request calls for opinion or legal characterization.
