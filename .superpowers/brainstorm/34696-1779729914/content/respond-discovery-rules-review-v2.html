Objection Rules

Vague, Ambiguous, Overbroad

Add when the request is unclear, too broad, speculative, or uncertain. Inserts the standard vague / ambiguous / overbroad objection.

Ambiguous Or Undefined Term

Add when a request uses a confusing, undefined, or unclear word or phrase. The agent identifies the term and inserts it into the objection text.

Relevance And Privacy

Add when the request seeks information that does not appear related to the claims, defenses, damages, or case facts, or invades privacy.

Burdensome / Oppressive

Add when the request asks for a large volume of information, has no reasonable time or subject limit, or would impose undue burden or expense.

Privilege / Work Product

Add when the request could seek attorney-client communications, attorney mental impressions, investigation strategy, or protected work product.

Expert Opinion / Legal Conclusion

Add when the request calls for expert analysis, a legal conclusion, or an opinion that should not be required in a lay discovery response.

Substantive Response Rules

Minimal Direct Answer

Answer only the question being asked, use as few words as possible, and avoid volunteering extra facts.
